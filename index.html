📘 Learn SIMS

Learn how integer subtraction works on the SIMS grid.

What is SIMS?

SIMS is a modified Cartesian coordinate system used to represent integer subtraction.

Understand the Axes

X-axis → Minuend

Y-axis → Subtrahend

Coordinate → Difference

Example

5 − 3 = 2

Find 5 on the X-axis and 3 on the Y-axis. Their coordinate is (5, 3).

Negative Integers

7 − (−3) = 10

Locate 7 on X and −3 on Y, then apply the subtraction rule.
